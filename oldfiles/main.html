PomPom — A Discipline-Based Pomodoro Timer

PomPom is a structured Pomodoro timer designed to build real focus — not flexible excuses. Inspired by the original time-boxing method developed by Francesco Cirillo, this system enforces commitment through uninterrupted work sessions and intentional breaks.

How It Works

  • 25-minute deep focus session
  • 5 or 15-minute structured break
  • Reset when you finish a task
  • No break manipulation, strictly follow allotted break time
  • Timers run with purpose

During focus mode, PomPom studies with you. During breaks, you recover properly. There is no half-work, no half-rest.

This timer is built for students and creators who want structure, discipline, and measurable progress — not just a countdown clock. Focus is a decision. PomPom helps you commit to it.
